Stores work a bit like gravity.

Every place exerts a pull on the people around it. The pull is stronger when a place is more attractive — bigger, busier, more appealing — and it fades the farther away someone lives.

For each neighborhood, the model adds up the pull of every competing place and splits the expected visits in proportion. A site that's large and close captures a big share; one that's small or far captures little.
